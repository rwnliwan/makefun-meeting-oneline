## The line

> The coffee went cold before anyone said anything new.

53 characters, one sentence, one line. The joke is a race the meeting
loses: a cup of coffee cools in minutes, and in all that time nobody in
the room said a single new thing. If nothing new was said, there was
nothing the meeting was for — the line replaces the whole hour.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that one line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else
runs, no network, no input.
